# OOG A008.P2 — Binary testing versus affine recovery

Intake date: 2026-11-24.
Status: ANALYTIC_PROOF_CANDIDATE (counterexample and sharp bound as reported by source); NUMERICAL (source-reported LP). No independent reproduction or novelty certification in this intake.

## Statement
Let the source be the probability simplex Delta_2 and target the full square [0,1]^2, with all affine [0,1]-valued effects allowed. Let p=(1/2,1/2,0), q=(0,1/2,1/2), K(z)=(z1,z1+z2). For E_t(r,s)=max_e(e(r)-t e(s)), the source claims E_t(p,q)=E_t(Kp,Kq)=1/2+(1-t)_+/2 for every t>=0. Yet no affine channel from the full square to the simplex recovers both p and q exactly.

## Witness and sharp error candidate
Label square corners A=(1,1), B=(0,1), C=(0,0), D=(1,0), with recovery images a,b,c,d. Affinity requires d=a+c-b. Exact recovery at the AB and BC midpoints forces b=(0,1,0), a=(1,0,0), c=(0,0,1), hence d=(1,-1,1), outside the simplex.

For u=(a+b)/2, v=(b+c)/2 and delta=max(TV(u,p),TV(v,q)), the source gives the lower-bound certificate
6 delta >= (1/2-u1)+(1/2-v3)+2u3+2v1 = 1/2+3(a3+c1)/2+d2/2 >= 1/2.
The proposed attaining corner images are a=(2/3,1/3,0), b=(1/6,2/3,1/6), c=(0,1/3,2/3), d=(1/2,0,1/2). Both midpoint errors equal 1/12.

The source reports a SciPy 1.17.0 HiGHS linear program under Python 3.12.14, with tolerance 1e-9 against 1/12. Reproduction: minimize delta over 12 nonnegative corner coordinates and delta>=0, impose each corner sum=1, a+c=b+d, and each midpoint coordinate deviation from its prescribed state in [-delta,delta]. For three outcomes TV equals maximum absolute coordinate deviation. Not rerun during intake.

## Limits
Only a fixed pair, not every pair of source states. Recovery must be positive on the entire square, not just the embedded triangle. No claim about quantum or physical ontology.
