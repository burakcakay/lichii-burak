# G04 — Exact Lie-algebra obstruction to a universal fixed linear mode split

**Status: ANALYTIC_PROOF_CANDIDATE**, algebraic argument independently checked in SymPy over exact rationals. No claim that the observation is new in the literature.

## Statement

For every positive mass triple, let \(c_i\) be the three two-vectors defined in [G03](THREEBODY-G03.md). Consider admissible positive-definite Jacobi shape Gram matrices \(Q\in\operatorname{Sym}_2^{++}\), their corresponding \(K(Q)=\sum_i g_i(c_i^\top Q c_i)^{-3/2}c_i c_i^\top\), and the family
\[
A(K)=\begin{pmatrix}0&I_2\\-K&0\end{pmatrix}\in\mathfrak{sp}(4,\mathbb R).
\]

**Claim:** \(\operatorname{Lie}\langle A(K(Q)):Q\in\operatorname{Sym}_2^{++}\rangle=\mathfrak{sp}(4,\mathbb R)\) of dimension 10.

## Proof

Write \(c_1=(a,0)\), \(c_2=(b,c)\), \(c_3=(-d,c)\) with all a,b,c,d>0. Their symmetric rank-one matrices \(c_i c_i^\top\) are linearly independent: in any zero linear combination the (2,2) entry implies \(\beta+\gamma=0\); the (1,2) entry gives \(c(b+d)\beta=0\); the (1,1) entry then yields \(\alpha=0\).

Thus the map \(Q\mapsto(\sigma_i=c_i^\top Qc_i)_{i=1}^3\) is a linear isomorphism on \(\operatorname{Sym}_2\). On \(Q>0\), each \(\sigma_i>0\) and \(\sigma_i\mapsto g_i\sigma_i^{-3/2}\) is a local diffeomorphism. Hence \(K(Q)\) ranges over a nonempty open set in \(\operatorname{Sym}_2\).

The Lie algebra generated by \(A(K)\) therefore contains all lower symmetric generators \(L(C)=\begin{psmallmatrix}0&0\\C&0\end{psmallmatrix}\), and the upper block \(U(I)=\begin{psmallmatrix}0&I\\0&0\end{psmallmatrix}\).
Their commutators yield
\[
[U(I),L(C)]=H(C):=\operatorname{diag}(C,-C),
\]
\[
[H(C),U(I)]=2U(C),
\quad
[U(B),L(C)]=H(BC):=\operatorname{diag}(BC,-CB)
\]
for any symmetric B,C. Products BC of symmetric 2×2 matrices span all \(\operatorname{Mat}_2\), so the Lie algebra contains all 3-dimensional U(Sym2), 3-dimensional L(Sym2), and 4-dimensional H(Mat2) blocks. These are exactly \(\mathfrak{sp}(4,\mathbb R)\). QED.

**Consequence:** No single constant real invertible linear change of the four Jacobi phase indices can universally split this entire family into two independent 2×2 oscillator blocks. The natural 4D phase-index representation is irreducible under this full Lie algebra. This does **not** prove global Newtonian nonintegrability or rule out nonlinear, trajectory-dependent, special-case or non-elementary solution methods.

## Trajectory-dependent forcing eigendirections

For \(K=\begin{psmallmatrix}a&b\\b&c\end{psmallmatrix}\) and \(K\) with distinct eigenvalues, its instantaneous mode-axis angle satisfies
\[
\dot\alpha=
\frac{(a-c)\dot b-b(\dot a-\dot c)}
{(a-c)^2+4b^2},
\quad
\dot K=-3\sum_i g_i h_i^5(c_i^\top M c_i)c_i c_i^\top.
\]
The scalar numerator is \([K,\dot K]_{12}\). It vanishes on exact equilateral Lagrange central shapes but is generically nonzero: one tested unequal-mass configuration produced \([K,\dot K]_{12}\approx-24.1603\) and \(\dot\alpha\approx-2.45618\), with finite-difference \(\dot K\) agreement at ~4e-9. This is an explanatory coupling diagnostic, not a chaos theorem.

## Next falsifiable research program

Define a dimensionless mode-mixing observable
\[
\mathcal C(t)=\frac{\|[K,\dot K]\|_F}{(\operatorname{tr}K)^{5/2}},
\]
where K>0 for collision-free configurations. Compare its time integral against (i) numerical finite-time Lyapunov growth and (ii) scattering-label changes over matched initial-data ensembles. Control for close-encounter distance and compare to naive proxies. There is **no evidence yet** that \(\mathcal C\) outperforms existing predictors.
