# G01: analytic reconstruction and a critical-value caveat
Status: ANALYTIC_PROOF_CANDIDATE. For ell=|J|>0 and scaled variables q=rho*s, p=y/sqrt(rho), j=sum_a s_a cross y_a:
J=sqrt(rho)*j; hence rho=ell^2/|j|^2 and dt=ell^3/|j|^3 d_tau.
Newton homogeneity gives (0.5|y|^2-U(s))|j|^2=E ell^2.
At circular central configurations omega^2=U(s)/rho^3, ell^2=rho U(s) and E=-U(s)/(2rho), so E ell^2=-U(s)^2/2.
The connected analysis reported DOP853 reconstruction and gauge-round-trip PASS; the full stand-alone G01 test source was not supplied here, and no independent rerun is claimed.
