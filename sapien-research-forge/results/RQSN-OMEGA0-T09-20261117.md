## Specific claims and boundaries

1. **T09.1, invariant-order no-go (short proof supplied in source):** If a finite `N>=2` coordinate system is invariant under all permutations `S_N`, no *unique strict total order* on those coordinates can itself be invariant under all permutations. Swapping any two elements contradicts order preservation. This is a standard symmetry argument, **not** a novel physics theorem.
2. **T09.2, static CSP with asymmetric records (elementary derivation):** With iid unbiased past bits `X_i`, independent Bernoulli noise `eta_i` of parameter `epsilon`, and registers `M_i=X_i XOR eta_i`, a register of `k` noisy copies satisfies `I(X_past;M)=k[1-h2(epsilon)]`. A separate future bit independent of the register has `I(X_future;M)=0`. Source reports `k=4, epsilon=.1 => 2.124017625642875` bits and an exhaustive 256-assignment check. **Scope:** only the specified measure and independence assumptions.
3. **T09.3, nested record support:** `D(R_t)={X_0,...,X_{t-1}}` is strictly increasing for append-only registers, yielding a partial/order-like record chronology. **Caveat:** dependency orientation, labels, and append-only memory are assumed; it is not derivation of time from unlabeled bits.
4. **T09.4, finite controller:** `M=E XOR eta; A=pi(M); E_next=E XOR A XOR W`, with uniform `E`, independent `eta~Bern(epsilon)`, `W~Bern(delta)`, and `epsilon,delta<1/2`. Among the four deterministic one-bit policies, `pi(M)=M` uniquely maximizes `P(E_next=0)=(1-epsilon)(1-delta)+epsilon*delta`. At `epsilon=.1,delta=.05`, source reports scores `0.5,0.5,0.86,0.14` and an eight-assignment enumeration. **Scope:** this one-step structural model, not a physical or conscious observer.
5. **T09.5–T09.7, additional source-reported finite constructions:** A stipulated Gibbs preference over policies; Bayesian prediction with eight noisy observations of a latent Bernoulli bias (source reports `0.9198442161152001` classification and `0.7519065296691201` next-symbol accuracy); and a time-reversal-symmetric mixture with zero signed global arrow but nonzero local absolute record arrow.
